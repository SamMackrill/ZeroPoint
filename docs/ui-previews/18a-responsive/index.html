UI 18a · Tablet, phone and laptop layouts: before and after

The plan's three tiers. Desktop (1280 px and up) keeps the resizable shell; from 1280 to 1439 px the rail starts collapsed to icons on the first visit (and at icon width the rail now always shows icons only, however it got there). Tablets (768–1279 px) use the overlay rail and inspector with the viewport filling the screen above the timeline and a dock that starts collapsed to its readout strip (tap any tab to open it); the split view stacks under 900 px. Phones (under 768 px) are a basic viewer: no split view, Compare or palette (Casimir's loupe and Light's pair glyph stay in Selection), and the inspector is a bottom sheet with Setup, View and Selection. On touch screens every control is at least 36 px. A collapsed dock now also opens from the tab that is already selected.
